About

Neural Layer, established in 2019, specializes in helping companies modernize their IT landscape and become true data-driven organizations. We empower informed and effective decision-making by leveraging the latest in Cloud, AI, and Big Data technology.

We design target state architectures and develop practical roadmaps that tackle technical challenges and address business needs. Our focus is on delivering an agile, cost-effective, and secure platform that empowers data-driven decision-making.

Our Core Services

Our core services cover the full journey of becoming a Data Driven organization.

Cloud migration

Neural Layer offers expert skills and capabilities for cloud migration. We assist with service design, development of service management processes, and help convert strategic objectives into portfolios of services and service assets.

Our practitioners can help with planning, design, service catalog, service levels, availability, capacity, IT service continuity, information security, and supplier management.

Architecture

Setup and/or improve the architecture governance process in order to guide large transformations. Our architects are TOGAF and Archimate certified.

Implementation

We help to accelerate the implementation of data driven solutions using a proven agile approach and methodology that will eliminate much of the risk.
